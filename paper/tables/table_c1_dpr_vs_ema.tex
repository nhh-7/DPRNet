% SUPPORTED CLAIM (now direct, no longer motivational): replacing the cross-batch EMA
%   center generator with per-image dynamic prototypes improves accuracy on both benchmarks
%   (Set5 +0.06 dB, Urban100 +0.11 dB) with lower seed-to-seed variance, under a matched
%   protocol.
\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Ablation C1 ($\times 4$): dynamic per-image prototypes (DPR, $=$ full model) vs.\ a
cross-batch history center with EMA update (EMA-center), both trained for $250$k on DIV2K
over three seeds. Values are mean$\pm$std PSNR(dB)/SSIM (Y channel) on Set5 and Urban100.
Under a matched protocol that differs \emph{only} in the prototype generator, dynamic
prototypes give the better reconstruction on both benchmarks with lower seed variance.}
\label{tab:abl_c1}
\begingroup
\scriptsize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.05}
\begin{tabular*}{\columnwidth}{@{\extracolsep{\fill}}llcc}
\toprule
Benchmark & Prototype generator & PSNR & SSIM \\
\midrule
Set5     & EMA-center             & $32.51\pm0.04$ & $0.8990\pm0.0004$ \\
Set5     & DPR (dynamic, $=$full) & $\mathbf{32.57\pm0.02}$ & $\mathbf{0.9000\pm0.0002}$ \\
Urban100 & EMA-center             & $26.76\pm0.06$ & $0.8052\pm0.0009$ \\
Urban100 & DPR (dynamic, $=$full) & $\mathbf{26.87\pm0.02}$ & $\mathbf{0.8085\pm0.0003}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular*}
\endgroup

\vspace{2pt}
{\footnotesize
\textit{Notes.} Both arms share the identical CATANet backbone and DPR interface and differ
only in how routing prototypes are formed: EMA-center maintains a cross-batch $k$-means-style
center with an exponential-moving-average buffer and a hard \texttt{argmax} label
(EMACenterRouter), whereas DPR forms prototypes from the current tokens by a soft,
assignment-weighted aggregation. Each variant is trained for $250$k iterations on
DIV2K with seeds $\{3407,42,1234\}$; we report the mean$\pm$std over the three seeds. Dynamic
prototypes improve PSNR on both benchmarks (Set5 $+0.06$\,dB, Urban100 $+0.11$\,dB) and exhibit
lower seed-to-seed variance, giving a direct controlled confirmation of C1.}
\end{table}
