\caption{Ablation A1 ($\times 4$): prototype query refine. PSNR(dB) mean$\pm$std (Y channel)
over three seeds. Enabling refine improves reconstruction on both benchmarks by
more than the seed variance, confirming that decoupling prototype generation from confirmation
(C2) is quality-positive; we retain it by default.}

\midrule

w/\ \ refine (default)   & $\mathbf{32.57\pm0.02}$ & $\mathbf{26.87\pm0.02}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular*}
\endgroup

\vspace{2pt}
{\footnotesize
\textit{Notes.} Each variant is trained for $250$k iterations on DIV2K with seeds
$\{3407,42,1234\}$ and reported as mean$\pm$std at the validation-best checkpoint. Enabling
refine yields the better PSNR on both Set5 ($+0.04$\,dB) and Urban100 ($+0.05$\,dB); because the
improvement exceeds the seed-to-seed standard deviation, it is attributable to the query-refine
switch itself. The comparison of dynamic prototypes against a history center with EMA update
(C1) is reported separately in Table~\ref{tab:abl_c1}.}
