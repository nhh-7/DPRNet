\caption{Dynamic Prototype Routing (DPR). \textbf{(a)}~The router runs in a single
pass: it embeds the tokens, forms content prototypes $P^{\mathrm{c}}$ from the
current tokens, confirms them with a learnable query bank through a gated
refinement ($\gamma$), and matches the tokens back to the confirmed prototypes $P$
with a bounded temperature $\tau$, yielding the ordered sequence $X^{\pi}$, the
confidence $x^{\mathrm{s}}$, and the label $b$ (Eqs.~\eqref{eq:assign}--%
\eqref{eq:sortkey}); the usage-balancing term acts on the score map $S$
(Eq.~\eqref{eq:balance}). \textbf{(b)}~IASA consumes $X^{\pi}$, $P$, and
$x^{\mathrm{s}}$: a local branch and a prototype global branch are combined under a
confidence-modulated gate $\beta$, and a soft prototype fallback gated by $\alpha$
stabilizes low-confidence tokens before the output is scattered back via $\pi^{-1}$
(Eqs.~\eqref{eq:iasa_gate}--\eqref{eq:soft_fallback}).}
\label{fig:dpr}
