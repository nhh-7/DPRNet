\caption{Routing cluster maps at $\times4$. Each pixel of the LR input is colored
by its assigned prototype index $b_n=\arg\max_m S_{n,m}$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:belong}),
reshaped from the original token order to $H\times W$, for three TAB blocks
($b_0,b_3,b_7$); the leftmost panel is the nearest-neighbor-magnified LR image for
reference. The maps follow image content rather than a fixed spatial grid:
repetitive building facades in Urban100 and high-contrast strokes in Manga109 are
grouped into coherent prototypes, and the partition becomes finer in the deeper,
larger-$M$ blocks. Colors encode prototype identity within a block and are not
comparable across blocks. This visualizes the same routing signal whose confidence
$x^{\mathrm{s}}$ is quantified in Fig.~\ref{fig:xscore_hist}.}
